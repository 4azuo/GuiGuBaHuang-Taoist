Taoist 6.2 is out! (v6.2.0, ModLib v2.3.00)

Thank you so much to everyone who supports the mod — your coffees keep it going, and the mod stays free for everyone.

For newcomers: Taoist is a hardcore overhaul of Tale of Immortal. A living world where NPCs grow, marry and fight beside you; a harsh economy with inflation, loans and a player market; towns with taxes, bounties and cultivator clans; families that carry your legacy after you die; long storylines with real battles; and tougher battles with a Damage Calc window. Almost everything can be tuned in S&M Configs.

What's new in 6.2:

• Choose your destinies — a new window at character creation with every innate destiny of the game and filters by quality, name and description. Take as many as you like; each one lowers your S&M score.
• Taoist × Dao of Creation (optional, when you play both mods) — 2 new storylines, The Nameless Art and For One Heir Only. Characters create martial arts of their own and teach them to you, and older storylines have new rewards.
• S&M Configs shows the options of Dao of Creation on a page of their own.
• Taoist wallpapers for the title screen, trainer teleport by area, windows that no longer open over each other.

Still new since 6.1:

• Clan elders — outsiders who guard a clan for a salary. They vote on its wars and fight in them... and a disloyal elder may flee, or betray its clan in the middle of the battle.
• Succession — when the head of a clan dies, a child (or a sibling, or the spouse) carries the clan on. A clan wiped out leaves its mansion in ruins.
• Faster months — NPCs grow by turns and the game saves in the background.
• S&M score balance, new windows for plots, clans and elders, and an option to hide the Damage Calc button.

Still new since 6.0:

• Body Refining — from Copper Skin to Undying Body. Train under waterfalls, in lava and thunder pools, and survive the body tribulation.
• Merit & Karma — good deeds bring blessings; evil deeds bring power, bounties and revenge. Deep enough in karma, you can even massacre a town.
• Artisan Dao — alchemy, forging, talismans, feng shui, herbs and mining with ranks, exams, masterpieces, commissions and apprentices.
• Dao Heart and Spirit Sense — the strength of your mind, against heart demons and ambushes, with a scan of everything around you.
• Estates — every town has its own map. Buy a house, grow it from a thatched hut to an immortal palace, build rooms, pay your house tax.
• 4 storylines of 6.0 (16 in all, 18 with Dao of Creation): Old Tie's Forge, The House on the Old Street, Ashes of the Town, A Heartbeat in the Stone.
