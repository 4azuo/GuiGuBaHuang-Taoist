Taoist 6.3 is out! (v6.3.2, ModLib v2.3.07)

Thank you so much to everyone who supports the mod — your coffees keep it going, and the mod stays free for everyone.

For newcomers: Taoist is a hardcore overhaul of Tale of Immortal. A living world where NPCs grow, marry and fight beside you; a harsh economy with inflation, loans and a player market; towns with taxes, bounties and cultivator clans; families that carry your legacy after you die; long storylines with real battles; and tougher battles with a Damage Calc window. Almost everything can be tuned in S&M Configs.

What's new in 6.3:

• Seven constitutions — Pill Body, Sword Bone, Vajra Body, Innate Spirit Body, Dragon Blood Body, Thunder Body and Venom Body. 10 tiers each, awakened by the way you cultivate; your realm caps the tier, and NPCs awaken them too.
• Potions that keep up — the Pill Body and your alchemy rank raise their recovery; a rare Nine-Turn Golden Pill makes every potion heal 10% of your max HP / MP.
• A new storyline, The Medicine King's Testament: an old alchemist, three missing pages, and a lasting gift for your potions.
• New windows for constitutions, artisan ranks and rebirth; slower monster growth; a new S&M page "Skills & pills".
• A Support button on the title screen — thank you to everyone who uses it!
