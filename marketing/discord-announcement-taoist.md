# Taoist v6.2.0 is out!

## ★ Choose your destinies
- A new window at character creation lists **every innate destiny of the game**, with filters by quality, name and description
- Take **as many as you like**, on top of the 3 you roll. Each one costs **3,000–8,000 S&M points**, so an easy start means smaller rewards

## ★ Taoist × Dao of Creation
*Optional: only when you play both mods*
- 2 new storylines:
  - **The Nameless Art**: an outcast invents a martial art, and the old sect wants it
  - **For One Heir Only**: a dying master, one last art, two candidates
- Characters **create their own martial arts** and teach them to you
- 9 endings of the older storylines now reward you with an art or Dao Essence
- Dao of Creation options have their own page in S&M Configs

## Also
- Taoist wallpapers for the title screen
- Trainer: teleport by area
- Windows of several mods no longer open over each other

## ⚠️ Before you play
Update **Fouru's ModLib** to **v2.3.00+**, put it at the **top of the mod list**, then restart the game.
