# Taoist v6.3.2 is out!

## ★ Seven constitutions
- **Pill Body, Sword Bone, Vajra Body, Innate Spirit Body, Dragon Blood Body, Thunder Body, Venom Body**: 10 tiers each, every tier a destiny with its own name
- They **awaken from the way you cultivate** (potions, battles with an art, treasures, herbs, lightning...): a rare chance first, then they grow with their points. Your realm caps the tier
- Some cultivators are born with one, and NPCs awaken them too

## ★ Potions that keep up
- The Pill Body and your alchemy rank raise the recovery of your potions in battle
- Rare encounter (realm 8+): the **Nine-Turn Golden Pill**, every potion then also heals 10% of your max HP / MP
- New storyline **The Medicine King's Testament** (from Golden Core): an old alchemist, three missing pages, a lasting gift for your potions

## Also
- New windows: constitutions, artisan ranks, trainee / merchant ranks, rebirth
- Balance: monsters grow slower by grade, MP blocks cost by the damage blocked, new S&M page "Skills & pills"
- Title screen: the other mods of the author, and a Support button (Ko-fi)
- Body Refining window no longer overlaps its texts; S&M bank fee capped at +1,000%

## ⚠️ Before you play
Update **Fouru's ModLib** to **v2.3.07+**, put it at the **top of the mod list**, then restart the game.
