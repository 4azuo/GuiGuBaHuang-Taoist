# Taoist — a hardcore rework of Gui Gu Ba Huang (鬼谷八荒 / Tale of Immortal)

[![Steam Workshop](https://img.shields.io/badge/Steam%20Workshop-Taoist-1b2838?logo=steam)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3161035078)
[![Nexus Mods](https://img.shields.io/badge/Nexus%20Mods-Taoist-d98f40)](https://www.nexusmods.com/taleofimmortal/mods/60)
[![Ko-fi](https://img.shields.io/badge/Ko--fi-support-ff5e5b?logo=ko-fi)](https://ko-fi.com/fouru)

A living world, a harsh economy, towns you can rule, families, lifelong storylines, tougher battles, new paths of
cultivation, houses of your own and cultivator clans with their elders.

Most features can be tuned or turned off in **S&M Configs** (title screen). The harder your settings, the higher your
S&M score and the better your rewards. If it gets too hard, combine it with other mods.

## Install

- **Steam Workshop** (automatic updates): subscribe to [Taoist](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3161035078)
  and to [Fouru's ModLib](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3385996759) (v2.2.36 or newer).
- **Manual**: the zips of the [latest GitHub release](https://github.com/4azuo/GuiGuBaHuang-Taoist/releases/latest)
  or [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/taleofimmortal/mods/60): copy each `Mod_xxx` folder of the zips into the
  `ModExportData` folder of the game.
- ⚠ **Move ModLib to the TOP of your mod list**, then **restart the game**.

## New in 6.1

**Clan elders**
- Outsiders of no kin who guard a clan: their realm follows the area, their number the clan's house. They are paid a
  monthly salary and a yearly offering
- They have no say in the clan's affairs, but they vote on its wars (the head has 3 votes) and fight in them
- Invite elders to your own clan, become the guardian elder of an NPC clan that trusts you, or resign. Declare war
  through the war council
- In a clan war the elders fight beside their clan in the real battle. A disloyal elder may flee, or even **betray its
  clan in the middle of the battle**, and is hunted down afterwards

**Succession**
- When the head of a clan dies, a son or daughter takes over (else a grandchild, a brother or sister, or the spouse).
  Play on as your child and you may head the clan yourself
- A clan wiped out leaves its mansion in ruins: another clan may take it as its seat, or it is split into plots for the mortals

**Also**: faster months (the NPCs grow by turns, the game saves in the background), a balanced S&M score, new windows
for the plots, the clans and the elders, an option to hide the "Damage Calc" button.

## New in 6.0

**Five new paths of cultivation** (buttons on the cultivation screen, each one can be turned off)
- **Body Refining**: Copper Skin → Iron Bone → Jade Marrow → Golden Body → Undying Body. Temper your flesh with the
  blows you take, training and medicinal baths; train under waterfalls, in lava and thunder pools, face the body tribulation
- **Merit and Karma**: good deeds bring merit (luck, gifts, kinder NPCs, softer lightning); evil deeds bring karma, bounties
  and revenge... and the evil way: **massacre a town** in a real battle and leave it in ruins for 10 years
- **Artisan Dao**: alchemy, forging, talismans, feng shui, herbs and mining as 6 crafts with ranks, guild exams,
  masterpieces, commissions and apprentices
- **Dao Heart**: the steadiness of your mind, shaken by betrayal and broken promises
- **Spirit Sense**: senses ambushes; scan your surroundings once a month (cultivators, herbs, ores, places)

**Estates**: every town has its own map of plots, mortals who live and grow, and a palace for the town master. Buy land,
build from a thatched hut up to an immortal palace, add rooms, pay the house tax. NPCs buy houses too.

**4 new storylines** (16 in all): Old Tie's Forge, The House on the Old Street, Ashes of the Town, A Heartbeat in the Stone.

## Error reports

ModLib can send the errors of the mod to its author (BugSplat) **only if you allow it** on the title screen (change it
later on the title screen: ModCreator → Error reports). Sent: the error and its stack trace, the last lines of the mod log (your user
folder, Windows account and player id removed), versions, loaded mod ids, language, an anonymous id. Never your saves
or your Steam account.

### Known issues

**1. The game closes ~2 seconds after starting / code mods never load (non-CJK Windows)**

MelonLoader's `version.dll` refuses to start when the game folder path cannot be written in the Windows ANSI code page.
Steam installs the game to `...\steamapps\common\鬼谷八荒`. That path works on Chinese/Japanese Windows, but on
English/European/Vietnamese locales (code page 1252, 1258...) the game exits immediately (exit code 0, no MelonLoader
log). If `version.dll` was deleted instead, the game runs but only mod *configs* load, and no mod DLL runs.

Fix (pick one):
- **Start the game through an ASCII junction** (no reboot):
  ```bat
  mklink /J "%LOCALAPPDATA%\ggbh-game-link" "C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\鬼谷八荒"
  ```
  Then in Steam → game → Properties → General → **Launch Options**:
  ```
  "C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\ggbh-game-link\guigubahuang.exe" %command%
  ```
  (If your Windows user name is not ASCII, put the junction somewhere like `C:\ggbh` from an Admin prompt.)
  `GuiGuBaHuang-ModLib\scripts\game\run-game.ps1` creates and uses this junction automatically.
- **Or change the system locale**: Settings → Time & language → Language & region → Administrative language settings →
  *Change system locale* → Chinese (Simplified) or Japanese, then reboot. This affects other non-Unicode programs.

Keep the junction path short: MelonLoader's assembly generator fails past 260-character paths.

**2. Hundreds of "does not contain a definition" / "type not found" build errors after a game update**

The code is usually fine. The projects compile against `<game>\MelonLoader\Managed\*.dll`, which MelonLoader
regenerates only when it runs after a game update (see issue 1). Run it once and rebuild:
```powershell
.\scripts\game\run-game.ps1 -UntilModLoaded -StopGame -Duration 900
```
